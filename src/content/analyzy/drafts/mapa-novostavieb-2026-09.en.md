21 September 2026 | ANALYSIS OF THE BRATISLAVA NEW-BUILD MARKET

# The Bratislava new-build market is growing in supply and in sales, prices are standing still

The last two months on the Bratislava new-build market brought stronger demand, price stagnation and an increase in the number of flats for sale. Sales rose from 346 to 397 flats against the previous period. The price level was unchanged and moved by 0.1% on a comparable sample of projects. The supply of flats for sale rose to 4 305.

As of 21 September 2026 the supply on the new-build market consisted, according to Residata's analysts, of 4 305 flats across 97 projects. Against the position 65 days earlier, supply rose by 173 flats or 5.5%, the comparison being computed on the sample of 74 projects we track at both dates.

The average price of these flats on offer reached 5 736 €/m² incl. VAT, which on the comparable sample of projects is a change of only 0.1%. It can be said that new-build prices are currently moving at a pace close to zero, and that differences in the average price are caused by the composition of supply rather than by movements in price lists. Over the last two months 447 flats were added to the offer at an average price of 5 311 €/m² incl. VAT, that is below the market level, which slightly lowers the average price.

The average absolute price of flats on offer stood at 387 873 € incl. VAT, while their average floor area reached 66.2 m².

## The development of supply and sales on the Bratislava new-build market

Supply grew and demand rose against the previous period. 397 flats were sold against 346 in the preceding 57 days. Sales were not distorted by an exceptionally high figure in one of the large projects — the strongest of them accounted for 46 flats. Taking that into account, demand genuinely rose against the previous period.

The average achieved price of sold flats rose slightly to 5 732 €/m² incl. VAT, matching the average asking price. Behind the rise in the price of sold flats is above all increased demand in Ružinov and Staré Mesto, which are dearer than the average — in Ružinov sales rose from 130 to 167 flats and in Staré Mesto from 32 to 36. The average absolute price of sold flats was 360 459 € incl. VAT at an average floor area of 62.3 m².

## A view on where the Bratislava new-build market is heading

Tomáš Kamhal, founder of Residata: "The Bratislava market is in a state where supply and sales are both rising and prices are standing still. Over the last two months 447 flats were added to the offer and sales rose by 14.7% at the same time. So the market is absorbing the new supply for now.

The first factor is supply itself. There are 4 305 flats for sale today across 97 projects, and the number is rising. As long as the pace of sales keeps up, developers have no reason to cut prices — and the data bears that out: the average price moved by 0.1% on a comparable sample of projects.

The second factor is demand, still constrained by interest rates and household purchasing power. The average flat sold cost 360 459 € and its average floor area rose to 62.3 m², so the buyers entering the market were going for larger flats.

Unless interest rates and household purchasing power change materially, we expect the current state to continue: supply will keep growing and prices will stay roughly where they are. What will be interesting to watch is the development of supply — further growth could in time start to push prices down."
