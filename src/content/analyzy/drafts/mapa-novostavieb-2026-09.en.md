MARKET ANALYSIS · The new-build map · 21 September 2026

# Half of Slovakia's new-build supply is in Bratislava, and prices outside it are a third lower

The Slovak new-build market consists of 7 351 available flats in 240 projects across 43 towns. Half the supply is in Bratislava. The median asking price is 5 314 €/m² incl. VAT in Bratislava and 3 676 €/m² incl. VAT in the rest of the country. The median flat price is 317 400 € in Bratislava and 219 900 € in the other towns.

As of 21 September 2026 the supply on the Slovak new-build market was 7 351 available flats across 240 projects in 43 towns. Bratislava holds 3 635 of them, or 49% of the whole supply; the remaining 3 716 are spread across 42 towns. The largest regional markets are Nitra with 455 flats, Trnava with 444, Košice with 390, Prešov with 299 and Poprad with 265.

The median asking price of these flats reached 5 314 €/m² incl. VAT in Bratislava and 3 676 €/m² incl. VAT in the rest of the country, or 31% less. After Bratislava the dearest are Košice at 4 563 €/m² incl. VAT, Žilina at 4 102 and Banská Bystrica at 3 928, followed by Trnava (3 833), Trenčín (3 827) and Nitra (3 494). The lowest unit price among the towns with a larger supply is Lučenec at 2 308 €/m² incl. VAT.

The median absolute price of a flat on offer was 317 400 € incl. VAT in Bratislava at 58.6 m², and 219 900 € in the other towns at 61.0 m².

## Supply and the pace of sales in Slovak towns

Since 16 May 2026, from when we have recorded the supply daily, we have registered 1 348 flats sold, 762 of them in Bratislava and 586 in the other towns. At that pace Bratislava's supply would clear in 20 months and the supply in the rest of the country in 28 months. The figures Bencont Investments published for Q2 2026 — a supply of 4 231 flats and 652 sales — imply 19 months for Bratislava.

Price lists changed 1 855 times over the last 90 days across 73 projects. 87.4% of the changes were increases and the median change was 2.0%.

## A view on where the market is heading

Tomáš Kamhal, founder of Residata: "The gap between Bratislava and the rest of Slovakia is about a third on the unit price and just under a hundred thousand euros on the flat. The second figure matters more to a buyer, because a mortgage is granted against the total price of the flat rather than against the price per metre.

Prices are not moving down for now. Of 1 855 price-list changes over the last three months, 87.4% were increases. When demand is weaker, developers extend the selling time instead of cutting the price — which is also why the pace of sales says more about this market than the price level itself.

Unless interest rates and household purchasing power change materially, we expect supply to keep growing and prices to stay roughly where they are. We will be watching the regional cities in particular, where the pace of sales is slower than in Bratislava."

## Method

Residata reads Slovak developers' price lists every day, flat by flat. This overview rests on 240 projects across 43 towns and on observations since 16 May 2026, that is 128 days.

**What is counted.** Available flats are those the developer lists as available; reserved and sold are excluded. Only projects whose price list we currently read are included, and towns with at least 5 available flats; the table shows towns with at least 40. Homes only — garages, parking spaces, cellars and commercial units are excluded from every figure. The median €/m² is computed on living area including VAT; these are asking prices. Bencont and BuiltMind publish a mean and work with usable area, so their unit price and ours are not directly comparable.

**Months to clear** is stock divided by the average monthly number of sales over the observed period; a sale is identified from a change of status in the developer's own price list. Reserved flats count as stock rather than as sales, and their share is higher outside Bratislava.

**A price change** compares the same flat with its own earlier price, at one reading per flat per day and a change of at least two hundred euros.

**Coverage check.** The rezido.sk register lists 512 Slovak projects, 192 of them sold out. A comparison against our catalogue on 21 September 2026 found 15 selling projects we do not track, holding 101 available flats, or 1.4% of supply.

**Check against published figures.** Bencont Investments reports a Bratislava supply of 4 231 flats for Q2 2026; on a comparable definition we get 4 305, a difference of 1.7%. Their average asking price per flat is 380 000 € and ours 387 873 €, a difference of 2.1%.
