21 September 2026 | QUARTERLY ANALYSIS OF THE BRATISLAVA NEW-BUILD MARKET

# The supply of Bratislava new-builds keeps growing, prices are standing still

The third quarter of 2026 on the Bratislava new-build market means stronger demand, price stagnation and a further increase in the number of flats for sale. At the current pace sales are heading for 735 flats against 652 in Q2 2026. The price level was unchanged and rose by 0.1% year on year. The supply of flats for sale rose to 4 305.

As of 21 September 2026 the supply on the new-build market consisted, according to Residata's data, of 4 305 flats across 97 projects. Against Q2 2026 supply rose by 74 flats or 1.7%, while year on year it rose by 336 flats or 8.5%.

The average price of these flats on offer reached 5 736 €/m² incl. VAT, which year on year is an increase of only 0.1%. It can be said that new-build prices have for some years been rising at a pace close to zero — a year ago the average price was 5 732 €/m² incl. VAT and a quarter ago 5 701 €/m² incl. VAT. Differences in the average price are caused by the composition of supply rather than by movements in price lists.

The average absolute price of flats on offer stood at 387 873 € incl. VAT, while their average floor area reached 66.2 m².

## The development of supply and sales on the Bratislava new-build market

Supply grew and demand grew with it. Over 72 of the quarter's 92 days 575 flats were sold, which at the same daily pace means 735 flats for the full quarter, against 652 in Q2 2026 and 638 a year ago. Sales were not distorted by an exceptionally high figure in one of the large projects — the strongest of them accounted for 46 flats. Demand therefore rose by 12.7% against the previous quarter.

The average achieved price of sold flats rose slightly to 5 745 €/m² incl. VAT, matching the average asking price. Behind the rise in the price of sold flats is above all increased demand in Ružinov and Staré Mesto, which are dearer than the average — in Ružinov sales rose from 130 to 174 flats and in Staré Mesto from 32 to 36. The average absolute price of sold flats was 352 365 € incl. VAT at an average floor area of 61.0 m².

## A view on where the Bratislava new-build market is heading

Tomáš Kamhal, founder of Residata: "Over the past year sales have held at around 666 flats a quarter, while supply over the same period rose by 8.5%. More flats are therefore coming onto the market than are leaving it, and the difference is accumulating.

The first factor is supply. It has reached 4 305 flats across 97 projects and keeps growing. As long as developers feel each other's competition and sales keep pace, the result is price stagnation — over the past year the average price moved by 0.1%.

The second factor is demand, constrained above all by interest rates and household purchasing power. The average flat sold cost 352 365 € at 61.0 m², which is a threshold a limited number of households can reach.

Unless interest rates and purchasing power change materially, the new-build market will go on marking time, demand will stay bounded and prices will stagnate. What will be interesting to watch is the development of supply — further growth could already cause a degree of nervousness."
