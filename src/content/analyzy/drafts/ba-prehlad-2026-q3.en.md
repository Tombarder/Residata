# Bratislava: the new-build market in Q3 2026

Bratislava new-builds offered 4 053 flats across 93 projects at the close of the period. 582 flats sold between 1 July and 23 September 2026. The average asking price reached 5 722 €/m² incl. VAT.

## Summary

- **Supply: 4 053 flats available.** The largest is in Bratislava II — 1 162 flats, or 28.7 % of the city's entire offer.
- **Sales: 582 flats between 1 July and 23 September 2026.** Most of them in Bratislava II (252 flats).
- **Price: 5 722 €/m² incl. VAT.** On the same panel of 60 projects it moved barely at all against the previous quarter.
- **Finished housing is a minority of the offer.** 572 flats sit in completed buildings, 14.1 % of supply; 67.3 % is under construction, and for 18.6 % the developer publishes no completion date at all.

## A view on where the market is heading

Tomáš Kamhal, founder of Residata: "The Bratislava new-build market is really five markets that behave differently, and the city average hides them.

What deserves attention is how little of the offer is finished housing. A buyer who wants to move in immediately is choosing from a considerably smaller market than the price lists suggest.

And a column you will not usually find in an overview like this one: for 18.6 % of the offer the developer publishes no completion date at all. That is information a buyer needs before the price."
